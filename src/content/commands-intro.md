The server's own commands for the YAGPDB.xyz bot. Type one as an ordinary message and press
Enter. Discord opens its list of slash commands as you type the `/`, but when nothing in it
matches, your message goes through as you typed it. Most commands work in any channel, but
please use the Bots channels for them.

In the examples, `<text>` is something you fill in and `[size]` something you can leave out.
The bot usually deletes your command once it has replied.
