The server's own commands for the YAGPDB.xyz bot. Type one as an ordinary message, in one of
the Bots channels, and press Enter. Discord opens its list of slash commands as you type the
`/`, but when nothing in it matches, your message goes through as you typed it.

In the examples, `<text>` is something you fill in and `[size]` something you can leave out.
The bot usually deletes your command once it has replied.
